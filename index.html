05 — A neighbourhood shop

Built to be
part of your week.

Oak & Steel started with a simple belief: a barbershop should feel like a place you want to come back to. So we built a calm, considered room in the middle of the city—warm oak, brushed metal, no ego.

We take the craft seriously, but not ourselves. Come in with a reference photo, a half-formed idea, or just trust us to keep you looking like yourself.
